# AgentReady Woo — 30s Ad Film Script + Generative Prompts

Tone: cinematic, warm, confident. NOT techy. The hero is a small store owner.
Music: soft piano → warm build → resolve. No voiceover until scene 4 (optional).

## Scene breakdown

### Scene 1 (0-8s) — The question
Visual: Close-up, a woman in a small wool-goods workshop (evening, warm lamp light)
asks her phone: "Black wool running shoes under $130 — find me the best one."
Prompt (Veo 3 / Runway Gen-4):
> Cinematic close-up, warm evening light, a woman in a cozy artisan workshop speaks
> to her phone: "Find me black wool running shoes under $130." Soft depth of field,
> 35mm, shallow focus, muted warm palette, subtle film grain.

### Scene 2 (8-16s) — The search / discovery
Visual: Abstract agent scan — glowing purple radar sweep over a dark map of
storefronts; one store lights up: "northwindwool.com — agent-ready ✓"
Prompt:
> Abstract data visualization, dark indigo background, a purple radar beam sweeps
> across floating storefront icons, one icon ignites with a green checkmark,
> particles converge, elegant motion graphics, premium fintech aesthetic,
> deep purple #7C5CFF accents on near-black.

### Scene 3 (16-24s) — The sale
Visual: The workshop owner's phone dings: "New order via AI agent — Black Wool
Running Shoes." She smiles, packs a shoebox. Receipt prints: "✓ Sold via AI agent."
Prompt:
> Warm cinematic scene, small business owner receives a phone notification
> "New order via AI agent", smiles, packs a craft shoebox, a receipt printer
> types out a slip. Golden hour light, handheld camera, authentic documentary feel.

### Scene 4 (24-30s) — The numbers + CTA
Visual: Bold type on dark: "+38% purchase completion from AI referrals" →
"Set up in 5 minutes" → logo: AgentReady — Be found by AI shoppers. →
"Free scan · agentready.utilityhouse.xyz"
Prompt:
> Minimal motion graphics, dark indigo background, large serif typography animates
> in: "+38% purchase completion from AI referrals" then "5-minute setup" then
> logo reveal with soft purple glow. Premium, restrained, Apple-keynote style.

## Production notes
- Generate scenes separately (8s each), cut on the music beats.
- Keep palette locked: warm amber (human scenes) vs indigo/purple (machine scenes)
  — the film literally transitions from human warmth to machine precision and back.
- Captions burned in (most views are muted).
- CTA end-card must hold 2 full seconds.
