One-time purchases — Commerce Readiness Packet $9, Pro $49

If a one-time purchase doesn't deliver what it promises, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. No forms, no interrogation. Neither the Commerce Readiness Packet nor Pro renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Agency subscription

  • Cancel anytime from your dashboard billing page or by email. Cancellation stops all future renewals immediately; your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • First purchase: full refund within 14 days of your initial payment.
  • Renewals: refundable in full within 7 days of the renewal charge if you forgot or didn't intend to renew.
  • After those windows, remaining time is not pro-rated — but cancelling always stops future charges.

How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@utilityhouse.xyz with your order ID (or reply to any Paddle receipt), or contact Paddle support directly using the link in your receipt.
  2. We process approved refunds within 5–10 business days to your original payment method via Paddle.

Statutory rights

If mandatory consumer law in your country (including EU/UK distance-selling withdrawal rights) gives you stronger protections than this policy, those protections apply. Nothing here limits them.

Abuse

We may refuse refunds for clear abuse — e.g., repeated purchase-refund cycling of the same report.
