06Objections, answered

The questions you'd ask anyway.

Is it safe to hand over API keys?

You issue a read-only REST API key inside your own WordPress admin, scoped to your store, and you can revoke it in one click whenever you want. AgentReady can read your catalog; it cannot write, refund, or touch customer data. No card information ever passes through the service — every checkout completes on your own site.

Which AI agents does this actually reach?

Any agent that reads MCP endpoints or agent discovery files: ChatGPT shopping surfaces, Claude, and independent shopping agents such as NorthCinder. As standards like ACP and WebMCP stabilize, your feed format updates automatically — that's the point of building on the spec, not a hack.

How is this different from an SEO plugin?

SEO plugins make you readable to Google's crawler. AgentReady makes you readable, in-stock-queryable, and buyable by shopping agents — a different file format, a different protocol, and a checkout path. Google sends humans; agents send buyers with their wallets already out.

What if I stop paying?

The free tier keeps your feed, discovery file and cart handoff live forever — you just go back to a top-10-product limit instead of your full catalog. You don't lose visibility.
